Dear Gautam,

I don't always know how to put everything I feel into words, but today I wanted to try. So I made this little corner of the internet just for you, hoping that somehow these words can tell you what my heart sometimes struggles to say.

When I look back at all the little moments we've shared, I realize that it's not always the big things that stay with me. It's the random conversations, the silly laughs, the little jokes, the memories that came unexpectedly, and those ordinary moments that somehow became special simply because you were a part of them.

You've become such a beautiful part of my story. There are so many moments connected to you that I know I'll always carry somewhere in my heart.

There have been days when everything felt easy and happy, and there have also been days when things felt a little difficult. But through all those different moments, the memories we've created still mean something very special to me.

Sometimes I wish I could pause certain moments and stay there a little longer. Sometimes I wish I could go back just to experience a particular laugh, conversation, or little moment one more time.

I don't know what every tomorrow will look like, and I don't want to pretend that life will always be perfect. But I do know that I'm grateful for the moments that brought us here and for the place you've had in my life.

If you ever wonder whether the little things mattered, they did. If you ever wonder whether the memories were special, they were. And if you ever wonder whether you made someone smile just by being yourself, you did.

I made this website because a normal message felt too small for everything I wanted to say. So I collected a few memories, a few words, and a little piece of my heart and put them here for you.

Thank you for being a part of so many beautiful moments. Thank you for the laughs, the memories, the conversations, and all those little things that might have seemed ordinary but meant more than you probably realized.

Gautam, there is something I really want you to know, something that sometimes I struggle to put into words...

I LOVE YOU SOO MUCHH. ❤️🥹

More than these little words can ever explain. More than I sometimes know how to put into a message.

You mean more to me than you probably realize. Even the smallest moments connected to you have found a special place in my heart.

And if someday you look back at this little website, I hope you don't just see pictures and words. I hope you see the feelings behind them — the happiness, the memories, the smiles, and all the love that went into making this little surprise for you. ❤️

I hope whenever you look at these memories, you smile a little. I hope you remember the laughs, the silly moments, the conversations, and all those tiny memories that made our time together special.

I don't need this little website to be perfect. I just wanted it to carry something real from me to you — my memories, my feelings, my gratitude, and a tiny piece of my heart.

Some people become memories.
Some become stories.
And some become a beautiful part of your heart that you carry with you. ❤️

With lots of love,

Hetvi ❤️
